@startuml
title Phishing Attack Flow (Educational)

actor Attacker
actor Victim
participant "Fake Website" as FakeSite
participant "Legitimate Service" as Service

Attacker -> Victim: Sends phishing email
Victim -> FakeSite: Opens malicious link
FakeSite --> Victim: Fake login page
Victim -> FakeSite: Enters credentials
FakeSite -> Attacker: Stolen credentials
Attacker -> Service: Attempts login
Service --> Attacker: Access granted (if credentials are valid)

@enduml
